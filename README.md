## The day's stories

- **Middle East / The Gulf (lead):** Iran strikes U.S. installations in Kuwait (Ali Al Salem base) and Bahrain with missiles and drones; Gulf states say the attacks were intercepted, after U.S. jets struck a Revolutionary Guards missile site on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Asia / Diplomacy:** Xi Jinping breaks protocol to greet Donald Trump on the tarmac in Shenzhen with a hug at the APEC summit; the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin three-way meeting is set for Thursday.
- **Middle East / Gaza:** Trump's Board of Peace faults Israel and Hamas for failing to meet their commitments, warning the Gaza ceasefire "cannot be sustained indefinitely."
- **United States:** Democrat Mary Peltola defeats Sen. Dan Sullivan in Alaska, giving Democrats a 56–44 Senate majority and becoming the first Alaska Native senator.
- **Europe / Ukraine:** The European Commission proposes a new €45 billion loan for Ukraine for 2027, to be repaid from future Russian war reparations.
- **Science / Space:** Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth — about 16 billion miles — the first human-made object to do so; its signals now take a full day to reach home.
- **Tech:** Nvidia's board rules out reinstating co-founder Jensen Huang as CEO, as the chipmaker reports record quarterly revenue of $115.8 billion.

Sources: Reuters, Associated Press, NASA / JPL, USA Today, YourStory — every
story above was verified from reporting dated 18 November 2026; no story from
an earlier day is presented as the day's news.

## How it works

`main.py` copies the pre-drawn `entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG) byte-for-byte to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). No network, no model calls,
no timestamps — the output is deterministic.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
